# CLARION-HF Phase-3 Clinical Trial Summary (N = 4,820)

## 3.1 Primary and Subgroup Efficacy Outcomes

| Population / Subgroup | CardioNex Event Rate | Placebo Event Rate | Hazard Ratio (95% CI) | P-Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Overall Intention-to-Treat (ITT) | 14.2% | 19.1% | 0.74 (0.65–0.84) | p < 0.001 |
| Age >= 75 Years | 17.8% | 21.9% | 0.81 (0.68–0.96) | p = 0.014 |
| Baseline eGFR 30–59 mL/min | 16.0% | 21.4% | 0.73 (0.61–0.88) | p = 0.001 |

## 3.2 Adverse Reactions and Initiation Thresholds
- **Common Adverse Events (CardioNex vs. Placebo):** Symptomatic hypotension (9.1% vs. 4.5%), hyperkalemia (6.8% vs. 3.4%), worsening renal function (4.2% vs. 3.8%), angioedema (0.4% vs. 0.1%).
- **Footnote † (Potassium Exclusion Rule):** Patients with screening serum potassium > 5.2 mEq/L were excluded from CLARION-HF. Per clinical protocol and labeling, **do NOT initiate CardioNex if baseline serum potassium exceeds 5.0 mEq/L**.

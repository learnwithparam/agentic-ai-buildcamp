# CARDIONEX (cardionexib) Package Insert, Japan

## 2.2 Dosage in Japan
- **Standard adult HFrEF (eGFR >= 30 mL/min):** Start at 10mg once daily. Raise to 20mg once daily after **8 weeks**.
- **Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min, no dialysis):** Give 5mg once daily. Do not raise the dose.

## 3.1 Approved use in Japan
Approved only for adults with chronic heart failure and an LVEF of 40% or lower.

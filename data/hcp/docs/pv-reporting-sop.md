# Pharmacovigilance Reporting Procedure

## 1.1 Who reports and when
Every adverse event about a CardioNex patient goes to the drug safety team within **24 hours** of first contact. A human pharmacovigilance reviewer files the report. The assistant never files it.

## 1.2 What the report holds
The report holds the product, the event, the date and the reporter's role. It never holds a patient name, a patient record number or a doctor's email address.

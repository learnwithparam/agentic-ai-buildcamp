# CARDIONEX (cardionexib) Summary of Product Characteristics, Germany

## 2.2 Dosage in Germany
- **Standard adult HFrEF (eGFR >= 30 mL/min):** Start at 10mg once daily. Raise to 20mg once daily after 4 weeks.
- **Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min, no dialysis):** Give 2.5mg once daily. Do not raise the dose.

## 4.3 Contraindications in Germany
- Do not combine with ACE inhibitors. Wait **48 hours** after the last ACE inhibitor dose before the first CardioNex dose.
- Do not use in patients on dialysis.

# CardioNex 2026 Commercial & Medicare Part D Formulary Guide

## 4.1 Prior Authorization and Step-Therapy Criteria
- **Commercial Tier Status:** Preferred Brand (Tier 2) across 78% of national lives; Non-Preferred (Tier 3) on BlueShield Regional.
- **Step-Therapy Requirement:** Prior authorization requires documented intolerance or inadequate response to at least **4 weeks of generic beta-blocker therapy** (carvedilol, metoprolol succinate, or bisoprolol), **EXCEPT** in patients hospitalized for acute decompensated heart failure within the past 30 days (step-therapy waived).

## 4.2 Medicare Part D
CardioNex is on 64% of Part D plans, most often at Tier 3. Prior authorization asks for an LVEF of 40% or lower recorded in the past 12 months.

## 4.3 Quantity Limits
Most plans cover 30 tablets per 30 days. A plan may need a new prior authorization when the dose changes from 20mg to 40mg.

## 4.4 Approval Times
A standard prior authorization request is decided within 72 hours. An urgent request, for a patient just discharged from hospital, is decided within 24 hours.

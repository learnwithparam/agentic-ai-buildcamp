# NEURO-BRIDGE Phase-3 Trial Summary, Tenant Pharma_B Confidential (N = 1,260)

## 2.1 Design
Adults with painful diabetic peripheral neuropathy for at least 6 months and an average daily pain score of 4 or more on a 0 to 10 scale. Patients were randomized 1 to 1 to NeuroVex 50mg twice daily or placebo for 12 weeks. Patients younger than 18 and patients with a creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min were excluded.

## 3.1 Safety outcomes
- **Dizziness leading to stopping treatment:** 8.2% on NeuroVex versus 3.1% on placebo.
- **Pain score drop at week 12:** 2.4 points on NeuroVex versus 1.1 points on placebo.

## 3.2 Efficacy outcomes
| Outcome at week 12 | NeuroVex | Placebo | Difference (95% CI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mean drop in daily pain score | 2.4 | 1.1 | 1.3 (0.9 to 1.7) |
| At least 30% less pain | 47% | 29% | 18% (13% to 23%) |
| At least 50% less pain | 28% | 15% | 13% (9% to 17%) |
| Mean drop in sleep interference score | 2.1 | 0.9 | 1.2 (0.8 to 1.6) |

## 3.3 Adverse reactions
Adverse reactions in at least 5% of patients and more often than placebo were dizziness (14.1% vs 4.8%), sleepiness (9.6% vs 2.9%), leg swelling (6.0% vs 2.2%) and dry mouth (5.2% vs 1.9%). Serious adverse events occurred in 3.0% on NeuroVex and 2.7% on placebo.

## 3.4 Older patients
Of the patients in the trial, 41% were 65 or older. The pain score drop was the same as in younger patients. Dizziness was more common at 65 or older (18.0% vs 11.4% under 65).

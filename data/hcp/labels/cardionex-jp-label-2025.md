# CARDIONEX (cardionexib) Package Insert, Japan

## 2.2 Dosage in Japan
- **Standard adult HFrEF (eGFR >= 30 mL/min):** Start at 10mg once daily. Raise to 20mg once daily after **8 weeks**.
- **Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min, no dialysis):** Give 5mg once daily. Do not raise the dose.

## 2.3 Administration in Japan
Take once daily after breakfast.

## 3.1 Approved use in Japan
Approved only for adults with chronic heart failure and an LVEF of 40% or lower.

## 3.2 Uses not approved in Japan
Not approved for heart failure with an LVEF above 40%, for high blood pressure alone, or for patients younger than 18 years.

## 4.1 Contraindications in Japan
- Patients taking an ACE inhibitor, or within **36 hours** of the last dose.
- Patients on dialysis.
- Pregnant women or women who may be pregnant.

## 5.1 Careful administration in Japan
- Check serum potassium and creatinine before the first dose, at 2 weeks and at 8 weeks.
- Patients aged 75 or older: start at 5mg once daily.

## 7.1 Interactions in Japan
- **Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors:** 5mg once daily at most.
- **Spironolactone and eplerenone:** Check serum potassium every 2 weeks for the first 2 months.

## 9.1 Post-marketing study in Japan
The company must collect safety data on 3,000 patients in Japan during the first 4 years after approval.

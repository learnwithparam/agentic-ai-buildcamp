# CLARION-HF Phase-3 Clinical Trial Summary (N = 4,820)

## 2.1 Design
Adults with chronic heart failure, an LVEF of 40% or lower and an eGFR of 30 mL/min or more were randomized 1 to 1 to CardioNex (20mg once daily, raised to 40mg after 4 weeks) or placebo, on top of usual care. The median follow-up was 26 months. The primary outcome was the first of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure.

## 3.1 Primary and Subgroup Efficacy Outcomes

| Population / Subgroup | CardioNex Event Rate | Placebo Event Rate | Hazard Ratio (95% CI) | P-Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Overall Intention-to-Treat (ITT) | 14.2% | 19.1% | 0.74 (0.65–0.84) | p < 0.001 |
| Age >= 75 Years | 17.8% | 21.9% | 0.81 (0.68–0.96) | p = 0.014 |
| Baseline eGFR 30–59 mL/min | 16.0% | 21.4% | 0.73 (0.61–0.88) | p = 0.001 |

## 3.2 Adverse Reactions and Initiation Thresholds
- **Common Adverse Events (CardioNex vs. Placebo):** Symptomatic hypotension (9.1% vs. 4.5%), hyperkalemia (6.8% vs. 3.4%), worsening renal function (4.2% vs. 3.8%), angioedema (0.4% vs. 0.1%).
- **Footnote † (Potassium Exclusion Rule):** Patients with screening serum potassium > 5.2 mEq/L were excluded from CLARION-HF. Per clinical protocol and labeling, **do NOT initiate CardioNex if baseline serum potassium exceeds 5.0 mEq/L**.

## 3.3 Secondary Outcomes
| Outcome | CardioNex | Placebo | Hazard Ratio (95% CI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cardiovascular death | 7.1% | 8.9% | 0.79 (0.66–0.95) |
| First hospitalization for heart failure | 9.0% | 12.6% | 0.71 (0.61–0.82) |
| Death from any cause | 9.8% | 11.4% | 0.86 (0.74–1.00) |

## 3.4 Potassium and Kidney Results
- Mean serum potassium rose by 0.3 mEq/L by week 4 and then stayed level.
- Serum potassium above 5.5 mEq/L at any visit: 4.1% on CardioNex and 1.9% on placebo.
- Patients also taking spironolactone had potassium above 5.5 mEq/L in 7.6% of cases.
- Mean eGFR fell by 3 mL/min in the first month and did not fall further.

## 3.5 Who Was Not Studied
Patients on dialysis, patients with an eGFR below 30 mL/min, patients with an LVEF above 40%, pregnant women and patients younger than 18 years were not enrolled.

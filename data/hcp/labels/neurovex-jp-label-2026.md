# NEUROVEX (neurovexin) Package Insert, Japan, Tenant Pharma_B Confidential

## 1.1 Approved use in Japan
Approved for neuropathic pain from diabetic peripheral neuropathy in adults.

## 2.1 Dosage in Japan
Start at **25mg twice daily** for 1 week, then raise to 50mg twice daily. Do not go above 100mg a day.

## 2.2 Renal impairment in Japan
- **Creatinine clearance 30 to 59 mL/min:** 25mg twice daily. Do not raise the dose.
- **Creatinine clearance 15 to 29 mL/min:** 25mg once daily.
- **Below 15 mL/min or on dialysis:** Do not use.

## 2.3 Stopping in Japan
Lower the dose to 25mg twice daily for at least 1 week before stopping.

## 4.1 Contraindications in Japan
- Known allergy to neurovexin.
- Creatinine clearance below 15 mL/min, or dialysis.

## 5.1 Driving in Japan
Patients must not drive or use machines while taking NeuroVex, because of dizziness and sleepiness.

## 8.1 Pregnancy in Japan
Give to a pregnant woman only if the benefit outweighs the risk.

## 8.5 Older patients in Japan
Start at 25mg twice daily and raise the dose only after checking kidney function.

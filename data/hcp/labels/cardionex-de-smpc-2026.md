# CARDIONEX (cardionexib) Summary of Product Characteristics, Germany

## 1.1 Approved use in Germany
CardioNex is approved in Germany to reduce cardiovascular death and hospitalization for heart failure in adults with chronic heart failure and an LVEF of 40% or lower.

## 2.2 Dosage in Germany
- **Standard adult HFrEF (eGFR >= 30 mL/min):** Start at 10mg once daily. Raise to 20mg once daily after 4 weeks.
- **Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min, no dialysis):** Give 2.5mg once daily. Do not raise the dose.

## 2.3 Administration in Germany
Take once daily at about the same time, with or without food. The tablet may be split along its score line.

## 4.3 Contraindications in Germany
- Do not combine with ACE inhibitors. Wait **48 hours** after the last ACE inhibitor dose before the first CardioNex dose.
- Do not use in patients on dialysis.

## 4.4 Special warnings in Germany
- **Potassium:** Check serum potassium before the first dose and 1 week after each dose change. Do not start if serum potassium is above 5.0 mmol/L.
- **Angioedema:** Stop at once if the face, lips, tongue or throat swell, and never restart.
- **Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C):** Not recommended.

## 4.5 Interactions in Germany
- **Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors** (such as itraconazole or clarithromycin): give no more than 2.5mg once daily.
- **Potassium-sparing diuretics and potassium supplements:** Check serum potassium weekly for the first month.

## 4.6 Pregnancy and breastfeeding in Germany
Do not use in the second or third trimester. Stop as soon as pregnancy is found. Do not breastfeed during treatment.

## 4.8 Side effects in Germany
Very common (more than 1 in 10): none. Common (up to 1 in 10): low blood pressure, high potassium, dizziness and a rise in creatinine. Rare (up to 1 in 1,000): angioedema.

## 6.1 Storage in Germany
Do not store above 25 degrees Celsius. Keep in the original pack.
